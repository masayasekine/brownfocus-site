Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

We collect no data

Brown Focus collects neither personal information nor usage data. There is no account, and the app contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (work and break lengths, noise kind and volume, notification options)
  • The timer state (the current phase and when it ends)
  • A record of completed and skipped sessions (phase, start time, end time)

All of this is stored only on your iPhone and is never transmitted anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it.

Notifications and Live Activities

The app uses local notifications and a Live Activity to tell you when a phase ends. Both work entirely on the device; no server is involved. You can change the notification permission at any time in iOS Settings.

Network

The app does not connect to the internet.
