Support & how to use

How to use

  1. Tap the big ▶︎ to start working. Brown noise plays while you work and the ring empties as time passes.
  2. When work ends, a chime and a vibration tell you so, and the break begins. The noise stops during breaks.
  3. After four work sessions (adjustable) you get a long break.
  4. Pause, skip and reset (hold) are the buttons below the ring. A skip can be undone within five seconds.
  5. The gear opens the settings: durations, noise kind and volume, notifications and screen.

FAQ

Why doesn't the noise start when work begins in the background?

iOS does not let an app that is not on screen start playing audio by itself. When a break moves on to work automatically, tap ▶︎ in the Live Activity on the Lock Screen or in the Dynamic Island, or open the app, and the noise starts. Noise that started while the app was open keeps playing with the screen off.

I get no notifications

Check that notifications are allowed under iOS Settings → Notifications → Brown Focus. The app asks for permission the first time you start working.

Silent mode

In silent mode the chime is skipped and only the vibration plays. The noise still plays (use the volume buttons to adjust it).

Can I use it with music from another app?

Yes. The noise mixes with other apps' audio. If a phone call interrupts it, the noise resumes when the call ends.

Noise kinds

Deep, standard and bright. Switching the kind in the settings previews it right away.

Where is my data stored?

Settings and records stay on your device and are never sent anywhere. See the privacy policy.
